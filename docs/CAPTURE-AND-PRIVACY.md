# Capture and privacy

## What runs while SSE is open

| Mechanism | Windows API | Why | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keyboard observer | `SetWindowsHookEx(WH_KEYBOARD_LL)` in SSE's own process | Notice physical presses and releases of your bindings | Swallow, delay, change or send a key. Events flagged as injected are ignored. |
| Mouse button observer | `SetWindowsHookEx(WH_MOUSE_LL)` in SSE's own process, only while a side button is bound or Settings is open | Notice physical presses and releases of the side buttons (Mouse 4 and 5) | Look at the cursor position or movement; swallow, delay, change or send a click. Events flagged as injected are ignored. |
| Foreground watcher | `SetWinEventHook(EVENT_SYSTEM_FOREGROUND / MINIMIZESTART)`, out of context | Only react while the game is in front; hide on focus change or minimize. A card you kept open stays while you click it or go back to the game, and hides when anything else comes to the front | Inspect another process beyond its window title and executable name |
| Cursor position | `GetCursorPos` once per qualifying press of the quick price key; then every 40 ms, only while you hold the key and its card is showing (not in **Press again to hide**) | Put the ring at the cursor and place the card (beside where the game's tooltip should be, by the cursor's side of the screen); notice you moving the mouse onto the card, to keep it open | Read the screen; run while the key is up or once the card is kept open; use a mouse hook for it |
| Clipboard change counter | `GetClipboardSequenceNumber` | Wait until the game's own copy has landed | Read content (it is a counter) |
| Clipboard read | `Clipboard.GetText`, once per qualifying press | Get the item text the game just copied. A binding that isn't the game's copy key, such as a side button, reads what the clipboard already holds: the last item you copied | Read again on auto-repeat or release; with `Ctrl+C`, read when the game copied nothing |
| HUD card | Topmost, layered window. Click-through and no-activate while you hold the key; once the cursor reaches it, it takes clicks (click-through removed) but stays no-activate, so clicking it never takes the focus from the game (a fullscreen game would drop out as if alt-tabbed). You close it with its ×. It slides in, flashes once and fades out on release; where it goes comes from the game window's rectangle (`GetWindowRect`, `GetDpiForWindow`), the cursor position at the press and the item's text, from which SSE guesses the size of the game's tooltip. With **Fixed spot**, dragging a card you kept open moves its own window (mouse capture on SSE's window) and saves the spot | Show the result over the game; tick stats on a card you kept open, and press its Search | Take focus, even when you click it; catch clicks before the cursor reaches it; look at the screen to find the tooltip; read or search anything when you tick a stat or drag it; stay open once another program comes to the front, or on minimize, lock or a resolution change |
| Press cues | Topmost, layered, click-through, no-activate windows only as big as the cue: a ring at the cursor when a press that may read is heard, before the read; a red ring and a one-line note for 1.6 s when the game copied nothing; a line from the cursor to where the card went | Answer each press at once, say when nothing was under the cursor, show where the card is | Take focus or clicks; read anything; show for auto-repeat, injected input or with another program in front; stay up once another program comes to the front, or on lock or a resolution change |
| Animation effects | `SystemParameters.ClientAreaAnimation` (`SystemParametersInfo(SPI_GETCLIENTAREAANIMATION)`), read when a card or a cue shows | With Windows' animation effects off, use less motion: no ring, slide or line, only fades | Change the setting or read any other value |
| Soft click (off until you switch it on) | `System.Media.SoundPlayer` (Windows `PlaySound`), playing a short tick SSE builds in memory | Answer each press with a sound too, for players who want it | Play a sound file from anywhere; send anything to the game |
| Guide lines (off until you switch them on) | Topmost, layered, click-through, no-activate window, with its own foreground watcher (same `SetWinEventHook` calls as above) | Show two route lines while the game is in front, or for a few seconds over SSE when you press Preview | Take focus, intercept clicks, open anything when you change zone, or stay up while the item card, another window, minimize or lock is in front. It knows your zone only because you pick it. |
| Price check window (`F3`) | Topmost window that takes focus while open; `SetForegroundWindow` hands focus back to the game when it closes | Tick stats and type numbers over the game | Send input to the game; stay open once you press `Esc`, press `F3` again or click back into the game |
| Stash picture (off until you switch on **Collect stash pictures** in Settings) | Windows.Graphics.Capture for the game's window only (`IGraphicsCaptureItemInterop.CreateForWindow`, a free-threaded frame pool with one buffer, one capture session), called through COM from SSE's own process; Direct3D 11 on SSE's own device to copy the frame into memory; `QueryDisplayConfig`, `GetMonitorInfo` and `GetDpiForMonitor` for the notes | One picture of the game window's client area per physical press of the stash key while the game is in front, saved for building stash reading ([#3](https://github.com/AutumnTrace/still-sane-exile/issues/3)). A click-through, no-activate note over the game says "Saved stash picture 7" for a few seconds | Take a picture on auto-repeat, injected input, release or with any other window in front, or with Right Alt on a keyboard where it is AltGr (so typing in the game's chat takes none); take a second frame for one press; keep the capture session open (it closes within milliseconds, before the picture is read back); capture other windows, the desktop or the Windows mouse pointer; turn off Windows' yellow capture border; save a picture that came back late (you switched away or pressed again) or black; read anything from the picture; upload it |
| Live prices (only for a confirmed live league with live prices on) | HTTPS `GET` requests to poe.ninja through .NET's `HttpClient`, on a timer | Keep the league's prices current: a few seconds after start, then hourly ([Network](#network)) | Run on a key press; send anything about you; ask again before poe.ninja's cooldown ends |
| Trade site search (only when you click Search on a live item, with "Search the trade site from SSE" on) | One HTTPS `POST` and one `GET` to `www.pathofexile.com` through .NET's `HttpClient`, cookies off | Find the cheapest listings like your item ([Network](#network)) | Run on a key press, a tick or a changed number, or in the demo; log in or send a cookie or anything about your account; read or show sellers' names; ask again before the site's wait ends |
| Notification-area icon | `Shell_NotifyIcon` for SSE's own icon, its tooltip and one notification, with a hidden window of SSE's own (never shown) that receives the icon's clicks and Explorer's "TaskbarCreated" message; `ChangeWindowMessageFilterEx` lets that one message through when SSE runs as administrator. The menu is Windows' own popup menu (`TrackPopupMenuEx`), after `SetForegroundWindow` on the hidden window, which Windows requires for a menu from the notification area | Show that SSE is running. With **Closing the window keeps SSE running in the notification area** and **Minimizing hides the window there too** on (the defaults, Settings → Updates and privacy), the main window's × and minimize only hide it, and the keys keep working. A double-click, or `Enter` on the icon, brings the window back: shown, restored and in front. Right-click for **Open Still Sane, Exile?** and **Quit**, which ends SSE. The first time the window goes to the notification area, a notification from the icon says where it went, once, without sound (clicking it opens the window); Windows shows it in the corner, which never takes the focus. After Explorer restarts, the icon is added again | Come to the front, or bring the main window there, without your click or key on the icon; show the notice while the game is in front (it waits for another time), or more than once; stay in the notification area after SSE exits (it is removed on exit, also when an update restarts SSE) |
| Text size | Reads `HKCU\Software\Microsoft\Accessibility\TextScaleFactor` at start and when Windows reports a changed setting | Scale SSE's windows with the Windows text size ([Accessibility](ACCESSIBILITY.md)) | Change the setting or read any other value |

## What is stored

- **Settings** (`sse.db`, SQLite) in `%APPDATA%\StillSaneExile`, or in `data\` beside the portable build. With **Fixed spot**, that includes where you dragged the card, as shares of the game window's width and height. Program files live separately in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\StillSaneExile` and are replaced on each update; uninstalling removes them but keeps your settings.
- **Comparison sets** you create by adding a comparable: the asks you typed and the item text they compare against.
- **Holdings** you typed in or imported on My wealth in an earlier version (My wealth is switched off for now; what was saved stays): league, tab, item id, variant, quantity and snapshot time, plus the file's name (not its folder) for an import. The CSV file itself is only read when you pick it, never copied or uploaded. Format: [Wealth CSV import](WEALTH-CSV.md).
- **Campaign characters** you add in the guide: the name you typed, where you said you are on the route (leg, zone, act) and each tick, skip or reopen with its time, so undo and resume work after a restart. For their build: every Path of Building code you read, each as a revision with the build's notes and the gem steps read from it, the ascendancies you have done, and which steps you ticked. Steps you typed and a guide link you saved in SSE 0.6 or earlier stay too (the link is no longer shown). Removing a character deletes all of it. A copy of the last route pack that passed its checks is kept in `route-cache\` in the same folder.
- **Live prices** for a live league with live prices on: each hourly poe.ninja refresh as one file under `prices\<league>\` in the same folder, the newest five per league, so prices still show offline. They hold poe.ninja's public prices and nothing about you. `provider-cooldowns.json` next to them remembers when poe.ninja or the trade site asked SSE to wait, and the rate limits the trade site announced, so a restart keeps waiting.
- **Stash pictures**, only while **Collect stash pictures** is on: each press of the stash key with the game in front saves `captures\<date>\<time>.png` in the same folder (the game window's client area, 8-bit; an HDR frame is reduced to SDR) and `<time>.json` beside it. The JSON holds what SSE notes by itself: capture time, SSE's version, the game window's client size and whether it has a title bar, the monitor's name and resolution, Windows display scaling, HDR on or off (and the SDR white level), the pixel format and how long the picture took. It also holds what you add in Settings → Stash pictures: tab type, currency subtab, in-game UI scale, a note and "held out". The pictures never leave your computer: SSE uploads nothing, and **Delete** removes the PNG and its JSON. Chat and character names may be visible in a picture: crop or blur them before you share one.
- **Not stored:** clipboard text that is not an item (discarded immediately), recent items and their trade site results (memory only, cleared on exit), key presses, mouse clicks, cursor positions, the guessed tooltip, screen pixels other than the stash pictures above. A frame is held in memory only until its 8-bit copy is made, and the copy only until the PNG is written. Nothing from the game's files, memory or logs is read, so none of it can be stored.

## Network

SSE makes three kinds of request itself. None sends anything about you, your account or your game, only the trade site search carries anything about an item (the search you set up for it), and each has its own switch in Settings.

- **The update check:** an anonymous call to GitHub's releases API for this repository at start and every 6 hours, plus the package download when there is a new version. Settings → "Check for updates automatically" turns it off.
- **Live prices from poe.ninja,** only for a live profile with a confirmed league and live prices on (the default once you confirm a league). A few seconds after start, unless the last refresh is under an hour old, and then every hour, SSE sends `GET` requests to `https://poe.ninja/poe2/api/economy/exchange/current/overview` and `https://poe.ninja/poe2/api/economy/stash/current/item/overview`, one per category: 12 exchange categories (currency, fragments, essences, runes, soul cores, idols, uncut gems, lineage supports, omens, delirium, expedition, verisium) and 8 unique categories, so about 20 requests. Each carries only `league=` and `type=`, and identifies SSE with the User-Agent `StillSaneExile/<version> (+https://github.com/AutumnTrace/still-sane-exile)`; no cookies, no account, nothing from your computer. Requests go one at a time, at least 5 s apart, honour poe.ninja's `Cache-Control` and `ETag`, and stop when poe.ninja says to (see [Provider etiquette](PROVIDER-ETIQUETTE.md)). Choosing a league adds one request for poe.ninja's league list (`https://poe.ninja/poe2/api/data/index-state`): when you pick "A live league" in onboarding, press "Get the league list from poe.ninja", or open Settings while live prices are on. Settings → "Live prices from poe.ninja" turns all of it off.
- **The trade site search,** only when you click **Search** on the quick price card or the price check of an item in a live league, with "Search the trade site from SSE" on (the default). Never on a key press, a tick, a changed number or a click on the **Corrupted** tag, and never in the demo. One click sends two requests to the official trade site: a `POST` to `https://www.pathofexile.com/api/trade2/search/poe2/<league>` carrying the search as JSON (the item's base or category, rarity, the stats and numbers you ticked, instant buyout or not, corrupted or not (no filter when a corrupted item's **Corrupted** tag is clicked off), sorted by price), then a `GET` of `https://www.pathofexile.com/api/trade2/fetch/<up to ten listing ids>?query=<the search's id>&realm=poe2` for the ten cheapest. They carry the same User-Agent as live prices and nothing else: no login, no session cookie (cookies are off, so the one the site hands out is never sent back), no account data. The site's answer includes each seller's account name; SSE never reads it, shows it or keeps it. Requests follow the rate limits the site announces in its `X-Rate-Limit-*` headers and stop when it says to wait (see [Provider etiquette](PROVIDER-ETIQUETTE.md#the-trade-site)). Results stay in memory with the item. Settings → "Search the trade site from SSE" turns it off.

A key press never makes a request: the quick price card and the price check show only the prices already on your computer until you click Search, and the HUD code has no network client. "Open this search on the trade site" asks Windows to open your default browser on the official trade site, only when you click it, with your search in the link: the item's base, rarity and the stats and numbers you ticked, nothing about you or your account. The filter ids SSE builds that link and its own search from ship with the app in `data/trade/trade-data.json`. "Copy search" puts the link and the filters on the clipboard as text. The pictures of the Divine, Exalted and Chaos Orbs next to prices, and the fonts, ship with the app: SSE never downloads a picture or a font. The campaign guide makes no requests: its route pack and the passive tree it draws builds on and its pictures (`data/tree/`) ship with the app, and the Path of Building code you paste is unpacked and read on your PC. SSE never opens, downloads or shows a guide page or a build site; a pasted link is refused, not followed. Screen pixels, clipboard text, stash contents and build codes stay local.

## Demo isolation

The demo profile reads `fixtures/` only, uses a fixed demo clock (so ages are the same on every run), and is labelled DEMO in the header, the price check and the HUD card. Its listings are synthetic: made up around each item from a fixed seed, so the same item gets the same listings on every run. A live profile has no synthetic listings: its prices are poe.ninja's league-wide estimates, and its listings come only from the trade site, when you click Search. `PriceBookGuard` refuses a synthetic price book in a live profile and any price book from another league. A live price snapshot is loaded only if the league written inside it is exactly the confirmed league, and a refresh that finishes after you switched league is dropped.
